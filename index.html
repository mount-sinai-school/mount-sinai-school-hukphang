Who we are

About Mount Sinai School

Mount Sinai School Hukphang was established in 2016 through the collaborative efforts of Hukphang Baptist Church and Hukphang Village Council. The school was founded in response to the difficulties faced by parents in sending their children to the nearby towns for education. Its purpose was to provide children with the opportunity to begin their educational journey close to home in a familiar and supportive environment at minimal fees

Our school values education, responsibility, respect and the growth of every learner.
